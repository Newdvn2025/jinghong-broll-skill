# B-roll prompt decisions

## Translate editorial intent into pictures

Prefer visual evidence over a literal illustration of narration.

- Context: location, time, scale, weather, workflow, social setting.
- Process: hands, tools, repeated actions, material changes, cause and effect.
- Detail: inserts, textures, screens without legible private data, reaction fragments.
- Transition: matching movement, foreground wipe, rack focus, environmental bridge.
- Emotion: posture and behavior first; facial close-ups only when the story needs them.
- Abstract concepts: use a concrete situation or contrast rather than floating symbols and generic particles.

## Prompt order

Write prompts in this order when it improves clarity:

1. subject and continuity anchors;
2. location and time;
3. shot size and composition;
4. one subject action with a clear start and end;
5. camera behavior;
6. lighting, color, texture, and realism;
7. stability and exclusion constraints.

## Coverage selection

Choose the smallest set that covers the edit. A useful default for a short spoken section is one context shot, one action/process shot, and one detail/emotional punctuation shot.

Vary shot size and screen direction intentionally. Avoid consecutive clips with nearly identical framing or motion. If the footage will sit under voice-over, keep visible speech out unless requested and use restrained subject movement for clean in/out points.
